# FEMMIE Admin

Internal dashboard for the LOVE FEMMIE team. It shows key metrics (members, plan mix, revenue, content, engagement) and lets admins manage member plans and roles, review subscriptions and payments, publish courses, articles and FEMMIE TV videos, approve rewards and referrals, moderate community posts and handle support tickets. Every admin change is recorded in an audit log.

Data is shared with the public LOVE FEMMIE Global app. Payments are simulated until Stripe is connected; dashboard figures reflect preview/sample data.
