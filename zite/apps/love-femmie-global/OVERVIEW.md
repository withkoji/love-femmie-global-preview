# LOVE FEMMIE Global

A premium global lifestyle, learning and streaming platform — "Learn • Nourish • Love • Grow • Earn". Public visitors explore the homepage, recipes and FEMMIE TV; members sign in to take Academy courses, pass lesson quizzes, track progress, earn reward points, save recipes, keep a watchlist and plan their daily routine.

Four membership tiers (Discover free, Essential $4, Plus $9.99, Elite $19.99) gate courses and videos. This is a preview: checkout is simulated, media and course content are demonstration samples, and rewards have no cash value. Real payments (Stripe), licensed video hosting, email and an internal admin dashboard are planned next.
